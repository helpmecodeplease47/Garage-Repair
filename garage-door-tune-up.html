Why is my garage door so loud?

Noise usually means parts are wearing faster than they should. These are the usual causes.

Worn metal rollers

Old metal rollers grind and clack in the metal tracks. Sealed nylon rollers are a lot quieter.

Dry hinges and springs

When nobody's oiled the door in a while, you get popping and squeaking, and the hardware wears out faster.

Tracks or springs out of balance

If the tracks are off or the springs have lost tension, the opener has to work harder. That's the groaning you feel through the wall.

What we do

What's in a tune-up

A tune-up is more than a can of spray. We balance the door, tighten what's loose, and replace the parts making the noise.

  • Nylon roller replacement We take out the loud metal rollers and put in sealed nylon ones. Of everything we do, this makes the biggest difference in noise.
  • Lubrication We use silicone and lithium grease that holds up in cold and heat on the springs, hinges, bearings and opener rail.
  • Tracks and hardware Bolts shake loose over time. We go over every hinge and bracket and line up the tracks.
  • Spring balance We check how heavy the door feels and adjust the torsion springs so the opener isn't straining.

Tune-up and repair warranty

Repairs we do on a tune-up visit, like new rollers or hinges, are covered for 1 year. If we replace springs, those are covered for 3 years, parts and labor. The warranty covers the original homeowner on residential doors only. It does not cover damage, misuse, or work by others.

Garage Door Tune-Up FAQ

Why is my garage door so loud?

The most common causes are worn out metal rollers grinding in the tracks, dry hinges and springs that need lubrication, and tracks or spring tension that are out of balance.

What is included in a garage door tune-up?

We lubricate the springs, hinges, bearings, and opener rail, tighten loose hardware and align the tracks, balance the spring tension, and do a 15 point inspection. If worn metal rollers are causing the noise, we can swap them for sealed nylon rollers.

Will nylon rollers make my door quieter?

Yes. Old metal rollers clack and rattle against the metal tracks. Swapping to sealed nylon rollers is usually the biggest single fix for noise.

Is a noisy garage door a problem or just annoying?

A noisy door is a sign of extra wear and tear. When tracks are misaligned or springs lose tension, your opener has to work harder, which wears out hardware faster. A tune-up fixes what's causing the noise.

Is there a warranty on tune-up repairs?

Yes. Repairs like new rollers or hinges are covered for 1 year, and springs we install are covered for 3 years, parts and labor. The warranty covers the original homeowner on residential doors only. It does not cover damage, misuse, or work by others.
