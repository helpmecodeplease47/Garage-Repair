Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about garage door repair in West Jordan & South Jordan.

How much does garage spring repair cost?

It depends on how heavy the door is and what kind of springs it has. Call and we'll give you a free quote over the phone.

Do you work in Daybreak?

Yes. Daybreak is close to us, and so are Herriman and the rest of the south valley.

Can you fix my opener, or put in a new one?

Yes. I prefer LiftMaster and Chamberlain, and I also install Genie and Craftsman. If yours isn't worth fixing, we can put in a new, quieter one.

Do you offer emergency repairs?

Yes. A door stuck open isn't safe to leave overnight. On weeknights I can often be out within a few hours. If not, we'll get you on the schedule. Call and I'll tell you how soon I can get there.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes. Springs we install are covered for 3 years, parts and labor. Openers come with the manufacturer warranty plus 1 year on my install labor, and I handle the warranty claim for you. New doors come with the manufacturer warranty plus 1 year on my install labor. Other repairs are covered for 1 year. The warranty covers the original homeowner on residential doors only. It does not cover damage, misuse, or work by others.
