Common Garage Door Repairs in South Jordan

Mixed-age homes, mixed hardware

Plenty of South Jordan homes have had additions, new doors, or opener swaps over the years, so parts don't always match. We check the springs, cables, drums, and rollers as a set and size replacement springs to the actual weight of your door.

Freeze-thaw and the bottom seal

Snowmelt that runs under the door and refreezes overnight can glue the bottom seal to the slab. If the opener strains on a cold morning, don't keep pressing the button; you can strip the gear or bend the top panel. A new seal and correctly set opener force prevent repeats.

Big doors on newer homes

Three-car garages and 16- to 18-foot doors put more load on springs, cables, and the opener. When springs on a heavy door need replacing, ask us about higher-cycle springs so you're not calling again in a few years.

HOA subdivisions

Many South Jordan subdivisions have HOAs with rules about exterior colors and door styles. If you're replacing a door, check your CC&Rs first; we'll provide the specs and color names for the approval.

South Jordan Garage Door FAQ

Do you serve South Jordan east of Bangerter, or only Daybreak?

Both. This page covers South Jordan's neighborhoods east of Bangerter Highway, and our Daybreak page covers the master-planned community on the west side.

Why did my garage door spring break in winter?

Springs wear out with use, and one near the end of its life is more likely to fail in cold weather. Many standard springs are rated for about 10,000 open-and-close cycles, which a busy household can reach in well under ten years.

Can you match a door my HOA already approved?

We can quote the same style and color when it's still available, or a close match. Your HOA has the final say, so get approval before ordering.
