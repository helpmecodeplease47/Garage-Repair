Draper: Valley Floor to Mountain Ridge

Draper sits at the south end of Salt Lake County, where the valley narrows at the Point of the Mountain. That gap is known for steady wind; it's the reason paragliders gather at the Point. For homeowners, the same wind pushes on garage doors, drives dust into the tracks, and finds every gap in the weatherstripping.

Up on Traverse Ridge, SunCrest sits well above the valley floor and often gets more snow and colder mornings than the rest of Draper. In the foothill neighborhoods near Corner Canyon, homes tend to be newer and larger, with wide doors and heavy spring loads. Around Draper City Park and the older center of town, homes span a wider range of ages and door types.

Draper keeps changing. The Point, the redevelopment of the former Utah State Prison site, is planned to add homes, offices, and parks near I-15. Whether you live near the Loveland Living Planet Aquarium, the Corner Canyon trailheads, or along 12300 South, we can come to you; Bangerter Highway runs from our West Jordan shop straight to Draper. Tell us which part of town you're in, and in winter whether you're up on the ridge, so we can plan the visit.

Draper Garage Door Trouble Spots

Wind at the Point

Gusts strain a door that's out of balance or has loose hinges, and a door knocked off its track can fall. An annual hardware and balance check matters more in Draper than in most places.

SunCrest winters

Snow, ice, and long cold stretches stress springs and openers, and winter is when everyone parks inside. A fall tune-up and a bottom-seal check before the first big storm prevent most stuck-door mornings.

Heavy doors on newer homes

Wide and tall doors on Corner Canyon-area homes carry heavy spring loads. Replacing both springs as a matched pair keeps the door balanced and protects the opener.

HOA neighborhoods

SunCrest and many other Draper neighborhoods have HOAs with exterior rules. Get the door style and color approved before you order; we'll provide the specs.

Draper Garage Door FAQ

Do you go up to SunCrest?

Yes. We serve SunCrest and the rest of Draper's 84020 ZIP code. In winter the roads up the ridge can be slow, so call early and we'll give you a realistic arrival time.

Why does my garage door shake on windy days?

A little movement is normal, but rattling, bowing, or a door that jumps in its tracks usually points to loose hinges, worn rollers, or poor balance. Those are tune-up fixes, and handling them early helps keep the door from coming off track.

Should I replace both springs if only one broke?

On a two-spring door, yes. Both springs have done the same number of cycles, so the second one is usually close behind. Replacing them together keeps the door balanced.

Can my opener work during a power outage?

Many current openers support a battery backup. If yours does, we can add one; if not, we'll show you how to use the manual release safely.
