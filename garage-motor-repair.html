Openers on the Truck

Signs your opener needs work

These are the opener problems we get called about most.

Grinding or loud noises

If the motor grinds or rattles, the gears inside may be stripped, or the chain or belt needs adjusting.

Motor hums, door won't move

You hit the button and hear a hum, but the door sits there. Usually that's a bad capacitor, a broken trolley or stripped gears.

Works sometimes, not always

If the door stops halfway or reverses for no reason, it's often the safety sensors being out of line. It can also be the circuit board or the force settings.

Opener and motor questions

Why does my opener hum but the door won't move?

A humming motor with a door that won't budge often points to a burnt out capacitor, a broken trolley, or stripped gears. It can also mean a broken spring, because the opener is trying to lift a door the springs are no longer holding up. Don't keep pressing the button; call us and we'll find the cause.

Should I repair or replace my garage door opener?

A lot of openers just need a repair. If yours is old, replacing it can cost less than fixing it. We'll look at it and give you a free quote both ways.

What opener brands do you work on?

I prefer LiftMaster and Chamberlain, and I also install Genie and Craftsman. We install belt drive, chain drive, smart Wi-Fi (MyQ), and wall mount (jackshaft) openers.

Which garage door opener is the quietest?

Belt drives. They're the quiet ones, which is why we suggest them when there's a bedroom over the garage. Wall mount openers are quiet too and keep the ceiling clear.

Is there a warranty on a new opener?

Yes. It comes with the manufacturer warranty plus 1 year on my install labor, and I handle the warranty claim for you. Opener repairs are covered for 1 year. The warranty covers the original homeowner on residential doors only. It does not cover damage, misuse, or work by others.
