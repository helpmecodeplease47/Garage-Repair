Herriman Homes, Herriman Weather

Herriman has grown quickly, and most of its homes are newer than those in the rest of the valley. Rosecrest, the city's largest master-planned community, spreads across more than 2,500 acres of Oquirrh foothills near Blackridge Reservoir. Herriman Towne Center surrounds City Hall, the library, and the J.L. Sorenson Recreation Center, while Anthem and the neighborhoods along the Mountain View Corridor keep adding new streets.

Newer doesn't mean trouble-free. Builder-grade springs are often rated for around 10,000 cycles, and a busy household can wear through that in less than a decade. Many Herriman homes built in the 2000s and 2010s have reached the age when springs, rollers, and original openers start to fail, often within a few years of each other.

Elevation matters too. Homes on the higher foothill streets toward Butterfield Canyon catch more wind and snow than homes down near Redwood Road. Wind pushes on wide doors, ice builds up at the threshold, and cold mornings put extra strain on worn springs. A door that was merely noisy in October can be the one that won't open in January.

Herriman Garage Door Issues

Wind and wide doors

Gusty days put real pressure on 16- and 18-foot doors. A door with loose hinges or worn rollers can bow, bind, or come off its track. Tightening hardware and replacing worn rollers keeps the door riding straight.

Ice at the threshold

Drifting snow and meltwater freeze along the bottom seal. We replace cracked seals and set the opener's force and travel so the door stops and reverses safely instead of forcing through ice.

HOA rules in master-planned communities

Rosecrest has a master HOA, and some sub-communities add their own. If you're changing your door's style or color, check with your HOA before ordering; we'll provide the specs.

Smart openers for commuters

Plenty of Herriman residents commute on the Mountain View Corridor or Bangerter Highway. A Wi-Fi opener lets you confirm the door is closed and open it for a delivery from your phone.

Herriman Garage Door FAQ

Do you service Rosecrest, Anthem, and Herriman Towne Center?

Yes, along with Blackridge, the Mountain View Corridor neighborhoods, and the rest of the 84096 ZIP code.

My home is only about 10 years old. Why did the spring break already?

Springs are rated by cycles, not years. A door opened and closed four or five times a day can use up a 10,000-cycle spring in six or seven years. Cold weather often finishes off a spring that was already worn.

Can wind damage my garage door?

Strong gusts can bend panels or push a door off its track, especially if it's out of balance or has loose hardware. If your door is bowed or off track after a windstorm, don't run the opener; call us to inspect it first.

Do I need HOA approval for a new door in Rosecrest?

Usually, if the style or color changes. Check with your HOA before ordering. Repairs that don't change the look, like springs or an opener, normally don't need approval.
