A Garage Door Company That Knows Riverton

Riverton still has the small-town feel many people move here for. The Old Dome Meeting Hall at Riverton City Park on 12600 South is modeled on the city's historic Old Dome Church, and the park's rodeo arena hosts the Riverton Rodeo that kicks off Town Days each summer. Around that core the city has grown in every direction, especially west toward the Mountain View Corridor and Mountain View Village.

That growth shows in garage sizes. Many Riverton homes have three-car garages, extra-tall bays, or detached shops, and some older lots still have room for trailers and animals. Taller and wider doors need heavier springs, longer cables, and openers rated for the load. Homes closer to Redwood Road and the Jordan River are more likely to have extension springs and doors that were never insulated.

From our West Jordan shop, Riverton is a short trip down Bangerter Highway, Redwood Road, or the Mountain View Corridor. If you live near Intermountain Riverton Hospital, Riverbend Golf Course, or the CR Hamilton Sports Complex, you're right in our regular service area.

Common Garage Door Repairs in Riverton

Oversized doors

Tall and extra-wide doors are heavy, and their springs and cables work harder on every cycle. We look at spring sizing, cable wear, and the opener's lifting capacity together, because an undersized opener on a big door wears out early.

Detached garages and shops

Detached garages and shop buildings often go years without attention. Dry rollers, loose hinges, and rusted bottom brackets are common. A tune-up catches the parts that are about to fail before a cable snaps.

Hot summers, cold winters

Riverton gets the full Salt Lake Valley range, from afternoons in the 90s to January mornings in the teens. Those swings expand and contract the door and its hardware, loosen fasteners, and dry out seals. Lubricating with a garage-door-rated product twice a year helps a lot.

Garages that double as gyms and workshops

If you work out or build things in the garage, an insulated door and a tight bottom seal keep the space much more comfortable in January and July.

Riverton Garage Door FAQ

Do you service garage doors in all of Riverton?

Yes, all of 84065, from the Jordan River Parkway on the east to the Mountain View Corridor and the newer neighborhoods west of it.

My shop door is oversized. Can you repair it?

Call us with the door's width and height and what it's doing. Many oversized residential doors use the same kinds of springs and openers as standard doors, just sized up, and we'll tell you up front if a job needs special-order parts.

How often should a garage door get a tune-up?

Once a year is a good rule, ideally in the fall before freezing weather. Doors that cycle many times a day, such as on busy family homes, home businesses, and shops, benefit from a check every six months.
