Local Service in Lehi

Lehi, from Main Street to Silicon Slopes

Lehi is one of the oldest cities in Utah. It was incorporated in 1852, and downtown is the Lehi Main Street Historic District on the National Register. Lehi Roller Mills has been making flour on Main Street since 1906, and every June the Lehi Round-Up brings the rodeo to the Lehi Rodeo Grounds and parades out of Wines Park.

Most of Lehi is a lot newer than that. The city grew from about 8,500 people in 1990 to almost 76,000 in 2020 as Silicon Slopes filled in around Thanksgiving Point and up on Traverse Mountain, and Holbrook Farms and the other neighborhoods near the Jordan River are still building. So Lehi has both kinds of garages: older doors and openers near the historic core, and newer insulated doors on torsion springs in north Lehi.

Coming from West Jordan, we take Bangerter Highway to I-15 and come over the Point of the Mountain. Thanksgiving Point and Traverse Mountain are about 25 minutes away, and Main Street is closer to 30 when traffic is light. Lehi has five I-15 exits, so tell us which one is closest when you call.

What we see in Lehi garages

Old openers near Main Street

Federal rules have required safety sensors or an edge sensor on openers made since January 1, 1993. If your opener is older than that, or the sensors are missing or tied together, we'd replace it. We install LiftMaster and Chamberlain and also work on Genie and Craftsman.

Busy households, more cycles

Lehi is a young town. The median age was about 26 in the 2020 census, and nearly 6 in 10 homes had kids under 18. More trips in and out means springs hit their cycle rating sooner. Our replacement springs carry a 15,000 to 25,000 cycle rating, backed by 3 years on parts and labor.

Cold mornings up on the bench

January lows in Lehi average in the mid teens, and homes on Traverse Mountain sit higher than the valley floor. Cold is hard on a spring near the end of its life, and old grease stiffens in the rollers. Before the first hard freeze, our 15-point tune-up checks the springs, rollers and balance.

Swapping out a builder door

If the builder put in a plain door and you want an insulated one, we can quote it by phone. We take the old door with us and recycle it, and you get the maker's warranty plus 1 year on our installation.

Lehi garage door repair FAQ

Do you really come to Lehi from West Jordan?

Yes. Lehi is part of our regular area, along with Utah County as far south as Spanish Fork. We cover both 84043 and 84048, from Main Street to Traverse Mountain and Holbrook Farms.

Is my old opener worth fixing?

Sometimes. If it's sensors out of line or a worn gear, we fix it. If it's old enough that new parts don't make sense, we'll tell you straight and quote a new opener by phone. No upselling.

What warranty do you give?

Spring jobs get 3 years on parts and labor. New openers and doors get the manufacturer coverage plus our 1 year labor warranty on the install. Everything else we repair is covered for 1 year.

What hours do you take calls?
