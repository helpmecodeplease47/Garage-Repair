Why people replace their garage door

These are the reasons we hear most.

It changes how the house looks

The garage door can be up to 30% of the front of your house. Swap an old, dented one and the whole front looks different.

A warmer garage

Utah winters get cold. An insulated steel door keeps the garage warmer in winter and cooler in summer, and it can help your energy bill.

Tired of fixing the old one

If you keep paying to fix the same old door, a new one can make more sense in the long run.

New Garage Door FAQ

Do you give free quotes on new garage doors?

Yes, and we come out to do it. We measure, go over the options with you and give you a price before anything is ordered.

What garage door styles can I choose from?

We install traditional raised panel steel doors (with or without insulation), carriage house style doors, and modern glass and aluminum doors. You can also pick from hundreds of factory colors and add decorative window inserts.

Is an insulated garage door worth it in Utah?

An insulated steel door keeps your garage warmer in the winter and cooler in the summer, and it can lower your energy bills.

Should I repair my old garage door or replace it?

If you keep paying to fix sagging panels, broken springs, and failing hardware, a new door often makes more sense. If the door is in good shape, a repair may be all you need. We'll tell you which one we'd do.

Is there a warranty on a new garage door?

Yes. New doors come with the manufacturer warranty plus 1 year on my install labor. The warranty covers the original homeowner on residential doors only. It does not cover damage, misuse, or work by others.
